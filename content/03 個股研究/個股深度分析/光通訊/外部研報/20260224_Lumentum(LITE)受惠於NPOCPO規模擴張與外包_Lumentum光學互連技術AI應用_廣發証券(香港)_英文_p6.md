Beneficiary of Scale-Up/Out NPO/CPO 
Maintain Buy and raise TP to $765: We see 2027 as a key inflection year for 
Lumentum, driven by accelerating CPO/NPO adoption across both scale-out and 
scale-up AI architectures. We expect total CPO/NPO laser revenue to reach 
~$124m/500m/1.7b in 2026/2027/2028, representing +389%/+305%/+245% YoY 
growth, and accounting for 3%/9%/20% of total revenue. In scale-out, ~80k 
NVIDIA CPO switches in 2027 could generate ~$273m in CW laser revenue for 
LITE, with additional upside from 3.2T ramps in 2028. On the scale-up side, 
potential CPO/NPO adoption in Rubin Ultra and future platforms offers a long-
term annual revenue opportunity >$1bn, with volume ramp starting late 2027. 
Higher structural gross margins further strengthen LITE’s position in next-gen AI 
interconnects. We revise up our FY26E/27E EPS to $8/$16 (CY26/27 EPS to 
$12/$22). Considering the strong scale-up CPO/NPO revenue contribution 
expected in 2028 and beyond, we apply a 35x PE multiple to our CY2027 earnings 
estimate with TP up to $765.  
Scale Out CPO switch ramp-up in 2027: NVIDIA outlined its scale-out CPO 
roadmap at GTC 2025, introducing Quantum-X (2H25) and Spectrum-X (2H26). 
While the first-generation 115.2T Quantum-X adopts a near-package optics 
(NPO) architecture and has seen limited adoption due to inferior cost-
performance benefits, we expect the next-generation fully integrated 115.2T CPO 
switch—leveraging advanced co-packaging support—to deliver materially 
improved thermal efficiency, bandwidth density and power efficiency. We model 
scale-out CPO shipments ramping to 20k/80k/150k units in 2026/2027/2028, with 
supply chain ramp beginning in 2Q26 and accelerating through 2H26/2027. We 
assume 65-70% market share and ~5k ASP per switch, translating into 
~$124/273/406m revenue in 2026/2027/2028, respectively.  
Scale Up CPO/NPO/OIO to drive laser content value: On the scale-up side, we 
see high chance for Rubin Ultra to adopt CPO/NPO architecture, due to the limits 
of copper cable. Hence, the inter-canister NVSwitch interconnect could carry 
several hundred dollars of CW laser content per GPU, in our view. We estimate 
that CPO/NPO adoption in Rubin Ultra and subsequent platforms could represent 
a long-term annual revenue opportunity exceeding $1bn, with volume ramp 
beginning in late 2027. We model Rubin Ultra shipments of 0.7m/5.0m units in 
2027/2028, generating ~$228m/$1.3b in 2027/2028, with scale-up becoming the 
primary growth driver from late 2027 onward. Looking further ahead, the long-
term evolution toward optical I/O (OIO) in next-generation architecture could drive 
incremental laser content, creating an additional structural growth vector. 
Importantly, regardless of whether the industry adopts NPO, CPO, or OIO, 
demand for high-power CW lasers remains indispensable, reinforcing LITE’s 
strategic positioning in next-generation AI interconnect architectures. 
Risks: (1) macroeconomic uncertainties; (2) weaker-than-expected performance 
in traditional businesses; (3) slower-than-expected AI momentum.
